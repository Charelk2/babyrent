Our Story

As local parents in beautiful Mosselbay, we know the joy and the challenges of traveling with little ones. We've juggled strollers through airports and wished for a familiar, safe crib in a new place. We started Mosselbay Baby Gear because we saw our friends and visiting families struggling with the same things. Our vision was simple: to provide high-quality, impeccably clean, and safe baby gear, so you can pack lighter and enjoy every moment of your Garden Route adventure.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make your family vacation as seamless and enjoyable as possible. We are committed to offering a convenient, reliable, and friendly rental service that feels like borrowing from a trusted friend. We handle the gear, so you can focus on making memories.

Our Commitment to Quality

Your child's safety and comfort are our top priorities. Every piece of equipment undergoes a rigorous multi-point safety inspection and is professionally cleaned and sanitized using baby-safe, non-toxic products before each rental. We only stock gear from trusted brands that we would use for our own children.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book gear? expand_more

You can easily book your gear directly through our website. Simply browse our products, select the items you need, choose your rental dates, and complete the checkout process. You'll receive a confirmation email shortly after.

What are your cleaning standards? expand_more

We take cleanliness very seriously. All our gear is thoroughly inspected, cleaned, and sanitized with non-toxic, baby-safe products after every use. Soft goods are laundered in hypoallergenic detergent.

Do you deliver to our accommodation? expand_more

Yes! We offer convenient delivery and pickup services to hotels, holiday homes, and private residences throughout Mosselbay and the surrounding areas. Simply provide your address during checkout.

What is your cancellation policy? expand_more

We understand that plans can change. We offer a full refund for cancellations made at least 48 hours before your rental start date. Please see our full policy on the FAQ page for more details.
