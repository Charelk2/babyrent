The Maxi-Cosi Coral XP is the first-ever integrated car seat and carrier nesting system that’s built to help parents move about effortlessly. The Coral XP revolutionary keep-close lightweight inner carrier (weighing about 5 lbs.) can be easily removed from the car seat’s outer carrier shell with the push of a button along with the use of the padded retractable side handles.

With a first-of-its-kind cross-body flex-strap, the Coral XP lets you go through your day with ease, so you can get things done, and pinch those cheeks and toes anytime you want. From car to home to wherever you want to be, the Coral XP is a revolutionary new system that makes carrying your little one easier.

  • Revolutionary 3-piece nesting system for a quicker, easier, and more lightweight way to get around.
  • Lightweight inner carrier, weighing about 5 lbs.
  • Features a first-of-its-kind cross-body flex-strap for hands-free carrying.
  • Four modes of carry: Cross-Body Carry, Handle Carry, Standard Carry and Stroller Carry.
